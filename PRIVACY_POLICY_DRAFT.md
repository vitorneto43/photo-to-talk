# Photo to Talk — Privacy Policy (draft for publication)

Photo to Talk is designed to capture or import photographs, recognize text and read that text aloud.

- Camera access is used only when the user chooses to capture a photo.
- Photos and recognized text are stored in the application's private storage on the device to provide a local history. The user can delete saved scans in the app.
- Text recognition is performed by on-device ML Kit models included with the app.
- Reading aloud uses the Android text-to-speech service selected on the device. Depending on the installed speech engine and its settings, speech data may be processed by that provider.
- Optional voice commands use a speech recognition service installed on the device. Depending on that service, recorded speech may be transmitted to the service provider for processing.
- The app itself does not operate an account system, advertising SDK or developer-operated server, and does not intentionally upload photos or recognized text to a developer-operated service.
- Device-level backup of app data is disabled in the manifest.
